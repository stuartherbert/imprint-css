## Introduction

### What Is The Miscellaneous Colors Collection?

The Miscellaneous Colors Collection is our collection of colors that don't (currently!) need their own dedicated Color Collection page in our docs.

### Where Did The Colors Come From?

These colors are (nearly) all taken from Wikipedia: either <ExternalLink name="Wikipedia List of Colors by Shade"/>, or:

* <ExternalLink name="Wikipedia Shades of Black"/>
* <ExternalLink name="Wikipedia Shades of Green"/>
* <ExternalLink name="Wikipedia Shades of Orange"/>
* <ExternalLink name="Wikipedia Shades of Red"/>

or from Wikipedia pages linked to those pages.

There are some exceptions: either where Wikipedia lacks a definition of the color, or where Wikipedia's definition seems to be an outlier:

* <ColorLink colorName="ansi-safetygray"/>
* <ColorLink colorName="ansi-safetygreen"/>
* <ColorLink colorName="ansi-safetypurple"/>
* <ColorLink colorName="munsell-green"/>

### Color Accuracy

Many of these colors are approximate. Their original definitions are either pre-digital, or are in color spaces that can't always translate perfectly to RGB colors.

It's not unusual for Wikipedia to revise the color definitions over time, either.

### Our Definition Guarantee

We're committed to keeping our color definitions the same once they have been published. We'll only change the color definitions:

1. to fix clear errors in the definition (ie fix typos in the color definition), or
2. if an "official" definition becomes available from the color's originator (e.g. ANSI publishes official RGB or CSS color definitions for the Safety colors).

You can use these colors knowing that they're going to still exist long into the future.

## Discussion

### Why No Pantone Colors?

We'd love to make Pantone's colors available in Imprint CSS. For many design professionals, they're the biggest name in color.

Unfortunately, <ExternalLink name="Wikipedia Pantone" text="according to Wikipedia"/>, Pantone asserts that their lists of color numbers and pigment values are the intellectual property of Pantone and free use of the list is not allowed.
