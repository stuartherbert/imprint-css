## Introduction

### What Is The Material Design Color Collection?

<ExternalLink name="Material Design v2" text="Material Design"/> is Google's design language for their Android operating system and for web-based applications.

When Material Design first launched in 2014, it came with a rich set of color palettes to choose from. In many ways, Material Design pioneered the adoption of color palettes in frontend frameworks.

While M3 (the version of Material Design launched in 2024) has moved away from the old 2014 color palettes, we think they're still relevant today - and still a great choice for anyone looking for colors for professional sites. That's why we've included them in Imprint CSS.

### How Did The Colors Get Their Names?

Google named all of the original <ExternalLink name="Material Design 2014 Colors" text="2014 color palettes"/>.

We've just added a `material-` prefix to keep the colors separate from any other color collections.
